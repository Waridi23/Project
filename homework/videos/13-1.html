Rock Climbing Fun

Rock Climbing Fun has recently opened a new school at the base of Mount Rainier. We have programs for advanced rock climbers as well as for beginners. Rock climbing is a widely popular sport, especially in the state of Washington. This is a sport that is for all ages and all levels of athletes.

Want to learn more about rock climbing techniques and safety? Visit the Mount Rainier National Park Climbing Guide.

Classes run from early morning to mid-afternoon, and private climbing lessons are available[cite: 1]. We even have weekend classes if that better fits your schedule[cite: 1].
