Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring to my first class?

Bring a water bottle, a small hand towel, and comfortable athletic clothing. Complimentary mat rentals and props (blocks, straps, bolsters) are provided.

Do I need prior yoga experience to join?

Not at all. All of our flow, Hatha, and restorative classes cater to practitioners of all experience levels with easy-to-follow guidance.

How early should I arrive before class starts?

Please arrive 10 to 15 minutes before class time to check in at the front desk, remove your shoes, and set up your mat quietly.

What is your cancellation policy?

You can cancel or reschedule your reservation free of charge up to 2 hours prior to class start time.
