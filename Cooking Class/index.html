Welcome to NextGen Culinary Lab

Whether you want to learn healthy weeknight meals, craft gourmet vegan dishes, or enjoy a romantic date night in front of the stovetop, our state-of-the-art kitchen labs have a seat saved for you!

Why Choose Our Lab?

  • Led by professional executive chefs and certified nutrition experts
  • 100% hands-on workstations with premium kitchen gear provided
  • Small class sizes (max 10 students) for personalized guidance
  • Take home full recipe cards and your freshly cooked feast

How Our Classes Work

  1. Browse & Book: Select a class style from our menu and lock in your reservation online.
  2. Arrive & Gear Up: Show up ready to cook! We supply all fresh ingredients, aprons, and equipment.
  3. Cook & Learn: Work through step-by-step master lessons alongside our executive instructors.
  4. Dine & Celebrate: Enjoy your culinary creations in our dining lounge with complimentary pairing drinks!
