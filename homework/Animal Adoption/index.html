How the Adoption Process Works

    Browse & Visit

    Browse our available dogs online or visit our shelter in person to meet them.

  2. 2

    Apply Online

    Fill out an adoption application to help us find your best personality match.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the adoption fee?

All adopted dogs are spayed or neutered, microchipped, and up to date on all vaccinations.

Can I bring my current pets to meet a dog?

Yes, we encourage bringing resident dogs to the center for a supervised introduction before finalizing an adoption.

What are your shelter hours?

We are open Tuesday through Sunday from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
